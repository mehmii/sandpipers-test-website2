Alkaline cleaners for laboratories and industry

The Sandpipers supplies RBS 25 Concentrate and RBS 35 Concentrate to customers in Singapore and Malaysia. Both remove grease, oils, proteins and mineral residues from glassware, metal parts, optics and electronics, and both rinse off without leaving a film.

What RBS concentrates clean

Used for manual cleaning, soaking and ultrasonic baths. Suitable for stainless steel, plastic, porcelain, quartz, ceramic and glass.

  • Laboratory glassware

  • Optical lenses and glass

  • Electronic parts and silicon wafers

  • Metal parts and injection moulds

Two products

Both are concentrated, foaming alkaline liquids made by Chemical Products s.a. in Brussels. They share a dilution rate and pack sizes, and differ in how they handle hard water and long soaks.

Cost-effective, wide range of uses

RBS 25 Concentrate

Multipurpose concentrated foaming alkaline cleaning agent for manual, soaking and ultrasonic cleaning.

High emulsifying and wetting power gives strong penetration in cleaning baths. It removes organic and mineral residues, greases, oils, distillation residues, proteins and cell cultures. In industry it degreases electronic components, silicon wafers, metal parts, injection moulds, glass and optical lenses.

How to use

Dilute 20 ml per litre of water, ideally at 50 °C. Keep soaking under 3 hours to avoid limescale deposits, or use demineralised water for longer soaks. Rinse straight away with tap water, or better, demineralised water.

RBS 35 Concentrate

Multipurpose concentrated foaming alkaline cleaning agent for manual, soaking and ultrasonic cleaning.

Built for high-performance cleaning where soaks run long or tap water is hard. In laboratories it removes organic and mineral residues, distillation residues, fats, ointments and cell cultures. In industry it degreases metal parts, moulds, lenses, glass and silicon wafers.

How to use

Dilute 20 ml per litre of distilled or tap water, ideally at 50 °C. Items can soak for extended periods in tap water without limescale deposits. Rinse straight away with tap water, or better, deionised water.

Appearance

White foaming liquid with a neutral smell.

Ingredients

Anionic and non-ionic surfactants, phosphates and polyphosphates, chlorinated agents, sodium hydroxide.

Pack sizes

5 L bottle, carton of 4 × 5 L, or 20 kg drum. Ask us about other sizes.

Handling

Do not mix with other products. Not recommended for white metals. Renew baths when pH drops or foam disappears.

RBS 25 or RBS 35?

Both clean the same materials at the same 20 ml per litre dilution. Choose RBS 35 if your tap water is hard or items need to soak for more than 3 hours. Choose RBS 25 for routine manual and ultrasonic cycles where cost matters most.
